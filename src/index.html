01 / Gallus

Farm management for layer-hen farms that keeps working when the internet doesn’t.

Gallus (FeedSmart) helps small Philippine poultry farms track flocks, daily egg and mortality records, feed and inventory. Staff record on an Android app, and the owner’s Windows desktop app runs the farm’s local sync server, with Supabase for sign-in and cloud backup.

  • Offline-first: local SQLite with queued sync that survives reconnects
  • Role-based access for owners, attendants, warehouse staff and supervisors
  • Daily and monthly reports with CSV export, plus weather and heat alerts
