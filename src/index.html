Gallus

Capstone project · Mobile + desktop

Desktop web demo Code

Farm management for layer-hen farms that keeps working when the internet doesn’t.

Gallus (FeedSmart) helps small Philippine poultry farms track flocks, daily egg and mortality records, feed and inventory. Staff record on an Android app, and the owner’s Windows desktop app runs the farm’s local sync server, with Supabase for sign-in and cloud backup.

  • Offline-first: local SQLite with queued sync that survives reconnects
  • Role-based access for owners, attendants, warehouse staff and supervisors
  • Daily and monthly reports with CSV export, plus weather and heat alerts

How I work

  1. 01 / Make it work

    I’ll chase a bug for hours, even days, until it gives. Getting a stubborn feature to finally work is the best feeling in the job.

  2. 02 / Make it feel good

    Once the hard part works, designing the UI and UX is my reward: the fun part, with nothing left to worry about.

  3. 03 / Back the lead

    I do my best work as the leader’s right hand. I take on the pressure and the details so they can make the hard calls.

  4. 04 / Own the design

    AI is great at syntax and testing, but it’s only as good as its instructions. Knowing what the system should be is my job, so I’m studying system design.
